What matters

The Fast Pass — the cheapest show-rate mechanic in the file

The confirmation video promises a free “Sales Career Fast Pass” guide gated behind three conditions: reply to the email with the words “I will be at the event”, show up on time on a laptop, and stay to the end — “that is how the fast pass activates and it gets sent to you right after.” One PDF buys a reply (which is also a deliverability signal), a micro-commitment, an on-time attendance and a stay-to-the-end. Cost to her: zero.

The bonus stack pays for a booking, not a sale

At 09:14 she promises $5,000 in bonuses for staying to the end. At 1:20:35 it becomes $10,000 — and the condition quietly changes from stay to book today. The slide totals seven bonuses at $9,979 and says “yours free when you book today.” Every bonus dollar is spent buying a calendar slot.

She refuses the price on purpose, out loud, and frames the refusal as service

Asked directly in the Q&A at 1:47:18: “we really have investment ranges that fit anyone’s budget… I would be doing you a huge disservice if we just had a checkout link right on here, because it’s not like that, it has to be customized and personalized to you.” Same play as 1 Percent Academy. Two competitors, same market shape, both hand the number to a human on the phone.

Proof is withheld from the registration page and dumped after the opt-in

The reg page has no testimonial, no logo, no number — just a countdown and a seat bar. The confirmation page then carries six full case-study interviews, 12–26 minutes each. Two of the six were published the day before capture, so the proof block is maintained continuously. The bet: proof does not buy the opt-in, it buys the attendance.

The whole class is a typed-commitment machine

Chat prompts are built into the deck roughly every ten minutes — type “I RELATE”, “I AM BREAKING FREE TODAY”, “I AM CLAIMING MY SOFT LIFE NOW” — and at the close “Let’s congratulate the ladies who have booked tonight! Type ‘I’M IN’ in the chat”, which converts other people’s bookings into live social proof.

The DIY teardown is the offer

One slide does the maths on doing it alone: 10 DMs a day, 2% reply, 50 companies needed to land one role, therefore 2,500 DMs and 12,500 minutes of unpaid work. The placement portal is then screen-shared with 200+ live roles at $6K–$20K/mo on-target. The arithmetic is the pitch.

The pre-call gate on the older VSL funnel — still the best single idea here

F047 serves “Your Call Is Booked, But Not Yet Confirmed” after booking, telling the lead to watch a video “to make sure we don’t cancel your call”, with the next step locked for 2:00. Commitment declared unfinished, loss framed on something just gained, and the video cannot be skipped.

Scarcity is asserted three different ways and never reconciled

“I have 18 spots and you only have the next 30 minutes” (1:26:26). Twenty minutes later, “my tech guy just messaged me, we only have two spots left” (2:01:57). Then “we have 113 women left, half of our ladies are gone” (2:06:53). Spot count, timer and room count all move independently.

Build hygiene is poor — read this before copying anything

The replay page still runs a dead script watching for an iClosed iframe and redirecting to flipland.info/pc or /dq497, with the embed URL app.iclosed.io/e/mentorme/land-flipping-strategy-call — a land-flipping funnel’s booking widget left inside the template. The replay headline links to preview--forged-site-analytics.lovable.app. Her Calendly slug is literally …-clone-2. The funnel converts anyway, which is the actual lesson.
