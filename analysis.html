Analysis

What they do, why it works, and what is worth taking.

Two funnels, one operator, one avatar that is ours. Ava Mistruzzi sells women the same escape we do — out of the 9-to-5, remote, no degree, no following — and then hands them a different vehicle. She places them on someone else’s offer as a closer; we teach them to create. Every mechanic below is transferable because the buyer is the same buyer.

1. The Fast Pass — steal this first

Three minutes of video on the confirmation page do the work that our setters currently do by hand. The lead is told to reply to the confirmation email with the exact words “I will be at the event”, then to show up on time from a laptop and stay to the end, and only then does the free guide arrive.

ConditionWhat it actually buys
Reply with “I will be at the event”A written micro-commitment, plus a reply to the sending domain — which is a deliverability signal for every mail after it.
Show up on time, on a laptopKills the phone-in half-watch. Desktop attendees stay longer and can click the booking link.
Stay to the endThe pitch is at the end. This is a stay-rate lever disguised as a gift.
Delivered after, by emailCosts one PDF and one automation. No discount, no bonus, no spend.

Our show rate is the metric we have spent the year on, and our current answer is follow-up volume. This is the opposite approach: make the lead do a small piece of work while intent is hot, and make skipping it forfeit something.

3. The price is refused, on purpose, on mic

At 01:47:18 she reads the question out and declines it: “we really have investment ranges that fit anyone’s budget… I would be doing you a huge disservice if we just had a checkout link right on here, because it’s not like that, it has to be customized and personalized to you.” The refusal is framed as care, and it is immediately followed by “that’s exactly why you book your call.”

No figure appears anywhere: not on a page, not on any of the 227 extracted slides, not in 20,213 transcribed words. 1 Percent Academy does the identical thing. Two independent competitors selling women a high-ticket escape both blackout the number until a human is on the line — that convergence is the signal, not the coincidence.

Read carefully

Not registered, and it did not matter. The webinar opt-in requires a phone number and we hold no research number, so the funnel was never opted into. The replay page is completely ungated, which is where the full 2h14m pitch came from.

Every price on this page was read off a slide, not off the transcript. Whisper renders each $1,497 as “14.97”, so a text search for the formatted number returns nothing on a transcript that states it four times.

Paid traffic is unresolved. She carries a Meta pixel and Hyros source labels, which is consistent with running ads, but no Her Closing Academy ads appear in the Gethookd library and the Meta Ad Library refused our requests. Do not claim she is organic-only.

Ignore two artefacts in her bundles. The iClosed / flipland.info block on /replay is dead template code from a land-flipping funnel, and the Calendly URL in shared.mjs belongs to the Inovat Framer template author. Neither is hers. Also, the capture engine reports “Typeform” on three pages — there is no Typeform, it is a Framer layer named typeform.
